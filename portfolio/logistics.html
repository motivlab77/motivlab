Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our logistics system

How does real-time tracking work?

Drivers use our mobile app which shares their GPS location in real-time. Customers can view this on a map with estimated arrival times and delivery progress.

Can I manage multiple vehicles?

Yes, you can manage unlimited vehicles and drivers. The system supports fleet management with individual tracking for each vehicle.

Is the driver app included?

Yes, our Standard and Premium plans include driver mobile apps for Android devices. Premium also includes customer mobile apps.

How accurate is route optimization?

Our route optimization considers traffic patterns, road conditions, and delivery priorities to create the most efficient routes, typically saving 20-30% on delivery times.
