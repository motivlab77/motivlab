Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our freelancer portfolio system

Can I update my portfolio myself?

Yes, all our plans include an easy-to-use admin dashboard where you can add new projects, update services, and manage content without any coding knowledge.

How does the booking system work?

Clients can view your available time slots, select a consultation time, and book directly through your website. You'll receive notifications and can manage bookings from your dashboard.

Can I accept payments online?

Yes, our Premium plan includes payment integration with Flutterwave and Paystack, allowing you to accept deposits or full payments for your services.

Is SEO included?

Yes, all our plans include basic SEO optimization. The Standard and Premium plans include advanced SEO features and our Premium plan includes an SEO consultation.
