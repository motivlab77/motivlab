Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our eCommerce platform

How long does it take to launch my store?

Typically 7-14 days from initial consultation to live store. This includes design customization, product upload, payment setup, and testing. We work quickly to get your store online and generating sales.

Can I manage products myself?

Absolutely! Our admin dashboard makes it easy to add, edit, and organize products. You can upload product images, set prices, manage inventory, and create categories without any technical knowledge.

Which payment methods do you support?

We support all major Nigerian payment methods: Flutterwave, Paystack, bank transfers, USSD, cash on delivery, and mobile money (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile). We can also integrate with international gateways like Stripe and PayPal.

Do you handle delivery integration?

Yes! We integrate with major Nigerian delivery services like GIG Logistics, DHL, FedEx, and local couriers. We can also set up your own delivery system with rider tracking and route optimization.

Is the store mobile-friendly?

Absolutely! Your store will be fully responsive and work perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and computers. We also offer mobile apps (Android & iOS) with our Premium plan for the best mobile shopping experience.

What about SEO and marketing?

All our stores include SEO optimization, social media integration, email marketing tools, and analytics. We also provide marketing training to help you promote your store effectively.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes, you can upgrade anytime. We'll migrate your data seamlessly to the new plan. Many of our clients start with Basic or Standard and upgrade as their business grows.

What ongoing costs should I expect?

After setup, you'll pay for annual hosting (starting from ₦40,000/year), domain renewal (₦5,000-₦10,000/year), and payment gateway transaction fees (typically 1.5-3.5% per transaction).
