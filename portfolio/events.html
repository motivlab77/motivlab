Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our event management system

Can I create multiple ticket types?

Yes, you can create unlimited ticket types with different prices, benefits, and availability. You can also set early bird discounts and promo codes.

How do attendees check in?

Attendees receive QR code tickets via email. At the event, you can scan these QR codes using our mobile check-in app or any smartphone.

Can I sell tickets offline?

Yes, our system supports offline ticket sales. You can manually add tickets sold offline, and they'll be synced with your online sales.

Is there a commission on ticket sales?

No, we don't charge any commission on ticket sales. You keep 100% of your ticket revenue (minus payment gateway fees).
