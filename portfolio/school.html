Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our school management system

How long does implementation take?

Implementation takes 7-14 days depending on your plan. This includes setup, data migration, staff training, and testing. We ensure everything is working perfectly before handover.

Can parents and students access the system?

Yes! Parents get their own login to view their children's results, attendance, fees, and communicate with teachers. Students can also access their portal to check results and timetables.

Do you provide training?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training for administrators, teachers, and staff. Training includes video tutorials, user manuals, and live support sessions.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely! We use enterprise-grade security with SSL encryption, regular backups, and secure hosting. Your data is protected with multiple layers of security.

Can I customize the system?

Yes! We can customize the system to match your school's specific needs, including custom grading systems, report card formats, and additional features.

What about ongoing support?

All plans include lifetime technical support. We're available via phone, WhatsApp, and email to help with any issues or questions you have.
