Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our school management system

How long does implementation take?

Implementation typically takes 7-14 days depending on your school's size and requirements. This includes setup, data migration, staff training, and thorough testing. We ensure everything is working perfectly before handover.

Can parents access the system?

Yes! Parents get their own secure login portal where they can view their children's results, check attendance records, pay fees online, message teachers, and receive school announcements. The parent portal is available on both desktop and mobile devices.

Do you provide training?

Absolutely. We provide comprehensive training for administrators, teachers, and other staff members. Training includes video tutorials, detailed user manuals, and live support sessions. We also offer refresher training as needed.

Is our data secure?

Yes, we use enterprise-grade security measures including SSL encryption, regular backups, secure hosting, and multiple layers of protection. Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized users with proper credentials.

Can the system be customized?

Yes, we can customize the system to match your school's specific needs. This includes custom grading systems, report card formats, admission workflows, and additional features. We work closely with you to ensure the system meets all your requirements.

What about ongoing support?

All plans include lifetime technical support. We're available via phone, WhatsApp, email, and support tickets to help with any issues or questions. We also provide regular system updates and maintenance to ensure optimal performance.

Does it work with existing school websites?

Yes, our school management system can be integrated with your existing school website. We can also build you a new website if needed. The system works seamlessly with your current online presence.

What happens after the hosting period ends?

We offer affordable annual hosting renewal options. You can also choose to host the system on your own server if preferred. We'll help you with migration and setup at no additional cost.
