Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our salon system

Can clients choose specific staff members?

Yes, clients can browse staff profiles and select their preferred stylist or therapist when booking. Each staff member's availability is shown in real-time.

How do reminders work?

The system automatically sends SMS and email reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before appointments. This reduces no-shows by over 80%.

Can I manage multiple salon branches?

Yes, our Premium plan supports multi-location management. You can manage staff, appointments, and reports across multiple branches from one dashboard.

Is training included?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training for you and your staff on how to use the system, manage bookings, and handle client relationships.
