Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our restaurant system

How long does setup take?

Setup typically takes 7-14 days depending on your menu complexity and customization needs. We handle everything from menu setup to payment integration and staff training.

Can I update my menu myself?

Yes! Our system includes an easy-to-use menu management dashboard where you can add, remove, or update menu items, change prices, and create special offers anytime.

Which payment methods do you support?

We support all major Nigerian payment methods including Flutterwave, Paystack, bank transfers, cash on delivery, and mobile money (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile).

Do you provide delivery rider apps?

Yes, our Premium plan includes a delivery rider app that allows your drivers to receive orders, navigate to locations, and update delivery status in real-time.

Can customers track their orders?

Absolutely! Customers receive real-time updates via SMS and email, and can track their order status from "Preparing" to "On the way" to "Delivered" on your website.

What about table reservations?

Our Standard and Premium plans include a complete table reservation system where customers can book tables, choose times, and make special requests online.

Is training included?

Yes, we provide comprehensive training for you and your staff on how to use the system, manage orders, handle reservations, and use the admin dashboard.

What ongoing costs are there?

After the initial setup, you only pay for annual hosting (starting from ₦30,000/year) and transaction fees for payment processing (charged by payment gateways).
