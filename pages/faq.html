General Questions

Basic information about MotivLab and our services

1
What is MotivLab and what do you do?

MotivLab is a Nigerian website development company specializing in creating custom websites with powerful admin dashboards for businesses across various industries. We help Nigerian businesses establish and grow their online presence with complete digital solutions.

Our services include:

  • Custom website development
  • Admin dashboard development
  • Mobile app development
  • SEO optimization
  • Website maintenance and support
2
What industries do you specialize in?

We have experience working with businesses across 8+ industries in Nigeria:

  • School Management Systems
  • Restaurants & Food Businesses
  • E-commerce Stores
  • Salon & Beauty Businesses
  • Real Estate Agencies
  • Logistics & Delivery Services
  • Healthcare Providers
  • Professional Services

We can also work with any other industry - our solutions are customized to your specific business needs.

3
How long have you been in business?

MotivLab was founded in 2020. In our 5+ years of operation, we've delivered over 200 successful projects for Nigerian businesses. Our team has combined experience of over 15 years in web development and digital solutions.

We've grown from a small startup to a trusted development partner for businesses across Nigeria, maintaining a 98% client satisfaction rate throughout our journey.

4
What makes MotivLab different from other web developers?

Several factors set us apart:

  • Complete Solutions: We don't just build websites - we build complete systems with admin dashboards for business management
  • Industry Specialization: Deep understanding of Nigerian business needs across different sectors
  • Lifetime Support: All our websites come with lifetime warranty on workmanship
  • Transparent Pricing: No hidden costs, clear package options
  • Fast Delivery: We deliver most projects within 4-6 weeks
  • Nigerian Focus: We understand the local market, payment systems, and user behavior
5
Do you offer free consultations?

Yes! We offer free 30-minute consultations to discuss your project requirements, answer your questions, and provide initial recommendations. During the consultation, we'll:

  • Understand your business goals and challenges
  • Discuss your website requirements
  • Provide ballpark estimates
  • Suggest the best approach for your needs
  • Answer any questions you have

You can schedule a consultation by calling us, sending a WhatsApp message, or using the contact form on our website.

6
Where are you located?

Our main office is located at:

123 Innovation Drive, Ikeja, Lagos, Nigeria

We serve clients across Nigeria and can work remotely with businesses in any state. For clients in Lagos, we're available for in-person meetings at our office. For clients outside Lagos, we conduct meetings via video call, phone, or WhatsApp.

Our office hours are Monday-Thursday 8:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Friday 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM, and Saturday 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM.

Can you work with clients outside Lagos?

Absolutely! We work with clients across Nigeria and even internationally. Most of our communication and collaboration happens online through:

  • Video calls (Zoom, Google Meet)
  • Phone calls
  • WhatsApp messaging
  • Email
  • Project management tools

We have clients in Abuja, Port Harcourt, Ibadan, Kano, Enugu, and other states. The distance doesn't affect the quality of our work or communication.

8
How can I see examples of your work?

You can view our portfolio in several ways:

  • Online Portfolio: Visit our Portfolio page to see case studies and demos
  • Live Demos: We have live demo websites you can explore with test credentials
  • Client Websites: We can share links to live websites we've built (with client permission)
  • Video Walkthroughs: Recorded demonstrations of admin dashboards and features

Contact us to request access to specific demos or to see examples related to your industry.

Pricing & Payment Questions

Information about costs, packages, and payment options

1
How much does a website cost?

Our website development packages start from ₦150,000. The exact cost depends on:

  • Package Selected: Basic (₦150,000), Professional (₦350,000), or Business (₦650,000)
  • Features Needed: E-commerce, booking systems, custom functionality
  • Number of Pages: More pages require more design and development time
  • Complexity: Simple brochure sites vs complex web applications
  • Add-on Services: SEO, mobile app, maintenance plans

We offer transparent pricing with no hidden costs. Contact us for a custom quote based on your specific requirements.

2
What's included in your pricing?

Our pricing includes:

  • Design: Custom design based on your brand
  • Development: Frontend and backend development
  • Testing: Quality assurance on all devices
  • Deployment: Setup on live server
  • Training: Training on how to use your website/admin
  • Support: Free support period (1-2 years depending on package)

Not included: Domain registration (₦5,000-₦10,000/year), hosting (₦25,000-₦50,000/year), and SSL certificate (₦10,000/year). These are separate costs that we can help you purchase.

3
Do you offer payment plans?

Yes, we offer flexible payment options:

  • Standard: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
  • 3-Month Plan: 40% upfront, 30% after design approval, 30% on delivery
  • Monthly Installments: Spread payment over 3-6 months (available for larger projects)

We accept bank transfers, online payments, and in some cases, credit/debit cards. We don't require full payment upfront for most projects.

4
Are there any hidden costs?

No, we believe in transparent pricing. All costs are clearly outlined in our proposals. The only additional costs you might encounter are:

  • Third-party Services: Domain registration, hosting, SSL certificates (we help you purchase these)
  • Additional Features: If you request features not included in your original package
  • Content Creation: If you need us to write content or source images (optional)

We'll discuss all potential costs upfront and get your approval before proceeding with any additional work.

5
What happens after the free support period?

After the free support period ends (1 year for Basic, 2 years for Professional, lifetime for Business), you have several options:

  • Maintenance Plan: ₦15,000/month for regular updates, security monitoring, and technical support
  • Pay-per-Support: Pay only when you need help (₦5,000-₦20,000 per issue)
  • Self-Manage: Take over maintenance yourself (we provide documentation)

Most clients choose the maintenance plan for peace of mind and to ensure their website stays secure and up-to-date.

6
Do you offer discounts?

We occasionally offer discounts:

  • Startup Discount: 10% off for registered startups (proof required)
  • Non-Profit Discount: 15% off for registered NGOs and non-profits
  • Referral Discount: 5% off when referred by an existing client
  • Bulk Discount: For multiple websites or large projects

Contact us to discuss if you qualify for any discounts. We also offer seasonal promotions - follow us on social media to stay updated.

7
What's your refund policy?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee with this refund policy:

  • During Development: Full refund of amount paid minus any third-party costs incurred if you cancel before project completion
  • After Delivery: 30-day money-back guarantee if you're not satisfied with the final product
  • Exceptions: No refunds for work already completed and approved, or for domain/hosting costs which are paid to third parties

We've never had to issue a refund because we ensure clients are happy at every stage, but the policy is there for your peace of mind.

Process & Timeline Questions

Information about our development process and delivery times

1
What's your development process?

Our 5-step process ensures successful project delivery:

  • Step 1: Discovery - We learn about your business and requirements
  • Step 2: Design - Create design mockups for your approval
  • Step 3: Development - Build your website and admin dashboard
  • Step 4: Testing - Rigorous testing on all devices
  • Step 5: Launch & Training - Go live and train your team

You'll be involved at every stage with regular updates and opportunities for feedback.

2
How long does it take to build a website?

Timelines vary by package complexity:

  • Basic Website: 2-3 weeks
  • Professional Package: 4-5 weeks
  • Business Package: 6-8 weeks
  • Custom Projects: 8-12 weeks (depending on complexity)

These timelines start after we receive the initial payment and all required content (text, images, etc.) from you. Delays in providing content can extend the timeline.

3
What do I need to provide?

To ensure timely delivery, we'll need:

  • Content: Text for all pages, About Us, services/products descriptions
  • Images: High-quality photos of your business, products, team
  • Brand Assets: Logo, color scheme, fonts (if you have them)
  • References: Links to websites you like (for design inspiration)
  • Functionality Details: Specific features you want (forms, integrations, etc.)
  • Credentials: Access to existing accounts (domain, hosting if applicable)

Don't worry if you don't have all these - we can help create content and source images for an additional fee.

4
How many revisions do I get?

We include generous revision allowances:

  • Design Phase: 3 rounds of revisions on the design mockups
  • Development Phase: 2 rounds of revisions on functionality
  • Final Review: 1 round of final tweaks before launch

Most clients are happy within 1-2 revisions. Additional revisions beyond these limits are billed at ₦10,000 per hour. We work closely with you to get things right the first time.

5
Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes! All our websites come with easy-to-use admin dashboards that allow you to:

  • Add/edit/delete pages and content
  • Upload images and documents
  • Manage blog posts and news updates
  • Update product/service listings
  • View contact form submissions
  • Manage user accounts (if applicable)

We provide comprehensive training and documentation. No technical knowledge is required - if you can use Facebook, you can manage your website.

6
Do you provide training?

Yes, training is included in all our packages:

  • Basic Package: 1-hour training session via video call
  • Professional Package: 2-hour training session (can be split)
  • Business Package: 3-hour training + documentation for your team

Training covers:

  • How to use the admin dashboard
  • Content management basics
  • Basic troubleshooting
  • Best practices for updates

We also provide written documentation and video tutorials you can refer back to.
