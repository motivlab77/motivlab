Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, process, and support

What makes MotivLab different from other web development agencies?

We specialize in building websites with powerful admin dashboards specifically for Nigerian businesses. Unlike many agencies that only build frontend websites, we provide complete solutions that include:

  • Custom admin dashboards for easy content management
  • Industry-specific features (school management, restaurant ordering, etc.)
  • Fast delivery (7-14 days for most projects)
  • Lifetime support included with all plans
  • Affordable pricing with flexible payment options
What industries do you specialize in?

We have extensive experience building solutions for various Nigerian business sectors including:

  • Education: School management systems, student portals
  • Restaurants & Food: Online ordering, table reservations
  • Retail: E-commerce stores, inventory management
  • Beauty & Salon: Booking systems, staff management
  • Real Estate: Property listings, agent management
  • Logistics: Delivery tracking, fleet management
  • Events: Ticket sales, event management
  • Professional Services: Portfolio websites, appointment booking
Do you work with businesses outside Nigeria?

While we primarily serve Nigerian businesses, we do work with clients across Africa and internationally. However, our pricing and payment options are optimized for the Nigerian market. We have experience building solutions that work well with local payment gateways, SMS services, and other Nigeria-specific integrations.

How long have you been in business?

MotivLab was founded in 2024 and has quickly become one of the leading web development agencies for Nigerian businesses. In our first year, we've delivered over 150 websites across various industries and maintained a 99% client satisfaction rate.

What's included in your pricing?

Our pricing includes everything you need to get your website up and running:

  • Complete website design and development
  • Custom admin dashboard
  • Hosting for the specified period (1-3 years depending on plan)
  • SSL certificate (on Professional and Enterprise plans)
  • Domain name setup assistance
  • Training on using your admin dashboard
  • Lifetime technical support
  • All source code and database
Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment options to make it easier for businesses to get started:

  • Standard Option: 50% upfront, 50% upon completion
  • Installment Plan: Spread payments over 3-6 months
  • Custom Arrangements: We can create a payment schedule that works for your cash flow

Contact us to discuss which payment option works best for your business.

Are there any hidden costs?

No hidden costs! Our pricing is completely transparent. The only additional costs you might have are:

  • Domain Name: ₦5,000 - ₦15,000/year (we can help you register this)
  • Additional Features: If you request features beyond your chosen plan
  • Ongoing Hosting: After the included hosting period ends (₦5,000/month with us)

We'll always discuss and approve any additional costs before proceeding.

  • Online transfers
  • Payment plans (as discussed above)

Our delivery times are much faster than industry standards:

What is your development process?

Our process is designed to be efficient and transparent:

  1. Consultation: We discuss your needs and requirements
  2. Planning: We create a detailed project plan
  3. Design: You approve the website design
  4. Development: We build your website and admin dashboard
  5. Testing: Thorough testing across devices and browsers
  6. Training: We train you on using the admin dashboard
  7. Launch: Your website goes live
  8. Support: Ongoing support and maintenance
