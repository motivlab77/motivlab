Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our pricing and services

What's included in the one-time payment?

The one-time payment includes website design, development, deployment, hosting for the specified period, and initial setup. It also includes training on how to use your admin dashboard.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes! We offer flexible payment plans. You can pay 50% upfront and the remaining 50% upon project completion. We also offer installment plans for larger projects - contact us to discuss options.

What happens after the hosting period ends?

After the included hosting period, you can continue hosting with us for ₦5,000/month, or we can help you transfer the website to your preferred hosting provider. Your website remains yours forever.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade your plan at any time. We'll apply a prorated credit from your current plan toward the upgrade. Many clients start with a basic plan and upgrade as their business grows.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes, we offer monthly maintenance plans starting from ₦25,000/month. This includes regular updates, security monitoring, backups, and technical support. All plans include lifetime basic support.

What if I need features not listed in the plans?

We specialize in custom solutions! Contact us with your specific requirements, and we'll provide a custom quote. We can build virtually any feature your business needs.
