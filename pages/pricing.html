Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our pricing

What's included in the quoted price?

The quoted price includes everything listed in the package: design, development, testing, deployment, and free support period. Hosting and domain registration are separate costs clearly mentioned for each package.

Do you offer customization beyond these packages?

Yes! We can customize any package to include specific features your business needs. Contact us for a custom quote based on your requirements.

How long does it take to build a website?

Basic websites take 2-3 weeks, Professional packages take 4-5 weeks, and Business websites take 6-8 weeks. These timelines start after we receive all required content and initial payment.

What happens after the free support period ends?

After the free support period, you can subscribe to our maintenance plan (₦15,000/month) which includes updates, security monitoring, backups, and technical support.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Absolutely! You can upgrade to a higher package at any time. We'll apply the amount you've already paid towards the new package.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not satisfied with our work during the development phase, you can request a full refund of the amount paid minus any third-party costs incurred.

All our websites come with a lifetime warranty on workmanship. If there's ever an issue with our code, we'll fix it free of charge.
